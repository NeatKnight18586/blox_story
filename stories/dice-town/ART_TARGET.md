# Dice Plaza — P1 art target

One shared opt-in WebGL scene, two fixed compositions. Original Roblox-inspired procedural art; no claim of official Roblox/UGC asset equivalence.

Warm ochre stucco, ivory ceremonial die, muted terracotta roofs, teal shop trim, cool slate shadows. Leo has a dark dimensional swept crop, teal long jacket, ochre strap and small notebook; supporting clothing is quieter. Canonical six-part oval-head silhouette and named pivots remain intact. Rounded architectural edges, arched windows, pitched roofs and striped awnings establish a shared town vocabulary before post effects.

A, frame 0: native 720×1280 elevated medium-wide; Leo foreground, result three on the die upper face plus physical rule plaque, four suspended supporting inhabitants, ceremonial paving and layered shops. A still demonstrates levitation staging, not a verified landing simulation.

B, frame 30: native 720×1280 three-quarter medium close-up; unobscured eyes/brows/mouth, directed toward the die, irritated brow choice, readable hair volume and jacket. Frame 60 offers cached focus; frame 0 surprise; frame 30 annoyance. These are isolated pose samples, not a new animation catalog.

Review at native size and 390 pixels wide. Compare actual S6 native g0 and g780 alongside A/B, clearly labeled as different scenes/cameras rather than matched before/after. Check identity, hair/face separation, prop scale, ground anchoring, lighting and result readability. Technical tests cannot award creative approval. Stop after two stills.

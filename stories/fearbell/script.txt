In this town, the Fear Bell spawns your *worst* fear.
Every morning, the Governor rings it.
Mr. Pickles' fear? *Heights.*
The mailman's fear? A *dinosaur.*
The strongest guy in town? A tiny *rat.*
And Kevin? Kevin is afraid of a *cheeseburger.*
Everybody's bell rings.
Mine never has.
So what's *my* fear?
At school, my teacher's fear crawls out of her desk.
Eight legs. Very hairy. Class is canceled.
At dinner, Dad's fear is the *vacuum cleaner.*
He eats standing on his chair.
But then Kevin did something weird.
He took one shaky *bite* of his fear.
And his cheeseburger got… *smaller?*
My little sister Lulu is afraid of the *dark.*
Every morning, a tiny shadow follows her around.
So I hold the flashlight.
She says I'm the bravest kid in town.
I'm not brave. I'm just never scared.
Then the rat guy started *petting* his rat.
Now it's a *mouse.*
Then the Governor rang the bell *too* hard.
It cracked.
The Governor said, "Oops."
And it rang again. At *noon.*
Every fear in town grew *GIANT.*
Lulu's shadow swallowed the whole plaza.
And Lulu was inside it.
Everyone ran *away* from the dark.
I punched the dark. Nothing.
I yelled at it. Nothing.
You can't fight somebody else's fear.
And I didn't know how to be scared.
So I walked in.
And for the first time ever… I was *scared.*
I found her hand.
"I'm scared too, Lulu."
She looked up at the giant dark.
And she said, "You're not that big."
The dark *shrank.*
Smaller. Smaller.
Until it fit in her palm.
Because a fear you face isn't your *worst* anymore.
Next morning, my bell rang. For the first time ever.
Something spawned right next to me.
A cheeseburger.

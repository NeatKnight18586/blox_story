In this world, everyone lives inside a headset.
Everyone except Nova.
Out here, the world is dust.
No grass. No birds. Just gray.
Nova sits outside every single day.
Watering the dust.
Nobody knows why.
Leo doesn't care.
Click. Blue light.
And he's everywhere.
Zombie city? He's the hero.
Dragon mountains? He rides one.
Space war? He's the captain.
In there, he has a hundred friends.
Every one in crazy armor.
Out here, he has one.
Tap. Tap. Tap.
The sky cracks open.
A giant finger pokes the zombies.
"Bro," his friend whispers.
"You're getting poked by the sky."
Leo knows that finger.
It's Nova.
He ignores it.
Space war.
Tap. Tap. Tap.
The finger boops his spaceship.
Straight into the sun.
Dragon world. Final boss.
The legendary sword is right there.
Tap. Tap. TAP.
The finger flicks the dragon.
The dragon cries.
Leo rips off the headset.
"WHAT, Nova? I was about to win!"
She doesn't say anything.
She just points down.
In the dust, there's something green.
One tiny blade of grass.
The first one in years.
Leo looks at her watering can.
"Every day?"
Nova shrugs. "Somebody had to."
"Now touch grass," she says.
Leo kneels down.
He touches it.
It's... kind of soft.
Behind him, the headsets come off.
One by one.
A whole town, staring at one blade of grass.
Bzzt.
"Achievement unlocked," says the headset.
"Touched grass."

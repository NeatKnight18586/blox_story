In Slime Town, you're born your *rarity*.
And only one in every *102* is born *Cosmic*.
Cosmics live up on Top Shelf Hill.
Everyone else waits down in the box.
Nobody has seen the real Cosmic in years.
Banana was born *Core*.
The plainest yellow in town.
Core slimes don't get tapped.
Core slimes get *traded*.
His only friend, Sunny, another yellow Core,
always told him the same thing:
Rarity's just *paint*.
So one night, Banana tried it.
He painted himself *Cosmic*. Purple, orange, blue, drip by drip.
And it *worked*.
The next morning, the giant hand reached down from the sky and tapped *him*.
*Click.*
Top Shelf Hill.
But that night, it *rained*.
Banana's paint started to run.
The whole hill gasped.
Yellow, dripping down his face.
Then Sunny came running up the hill through the rain, and stood right in front of him.
And *his* yellow was running too.
Underneath: purple, orange, blue.
Sunny was the *Cosmic*. The one in *102*.
Every night, he'd painted himself yellow just to sneak down to the box,
because the top of the hill was *lonely*.
'Rarity's just paint,' he said.
'I wanted a *friend*.'
The giant hand reached down one more time and tapped them *both*.
*Click.* *Click.*
One in 102 is *rare*.
A friend who paints himself plain for you?
That's *one of one*.

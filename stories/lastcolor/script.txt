In this world, every fall drains a dummy's *color*.
That's why they're all *white*.
I'm the last dummy with color left.
And I fall more than *anyone*.
Nine thousand falls, and counting.
On Monday, the giant fan blew me into the pool.
I landed in a *perfect* dive.
Nobody clapped.
My green pants turned *white*.
The white dummies don't remember their names.
They don't remember they ever *had* names.
Then a new dummy spawned.
Tiny, pink, and giggling at everything.
Her name tag said *Bitsy*.
She even followed me onto the roller coaster.
The cart flipped, and we both went flying.
Bitsy landed right on top of me.
I lost a color.
She didn't.
Her cheeks got *pinker*.
*Weird*, right?
On Wednesday, I got knocked out in the boxing ring.
By a dummy who kept saying sorry.
My blue stripe went white.
Bitsy held my hand the whole way back.
Then I saw something nobody else noticed.
A white dummy tripped onto a pile of dummies.
When he got up, he *blushed*.
Just a little.
Then a *player* joined.
His name floated above his head: BlastKid_7.
A giant hand came down from the clouds.
And it picked up *Bitsy*.
It carried her toward the launch tower.
I jumped in front of her.
So it threw *me* instead.
My jacket turned white.
It threw me again.
My hands turned white.
Again.
Now only my face was yellow.
One more fall, and I'd forget her.
I'd forget my own *name*.
Then the hand grabbed Bitsy again.
Up, up, to the very top of the tower.
And it let go.
I've fallen nine thousand times... so I know one thing.
A fall only counts when you hit the *ground*.
I jumped.
I caught her in the air.
I yelled the one thing dummies never forget.
"*Dummies help each other!*"
Every white dummy in the park started running.
Hundreds of them.
They piled up under the tower like a giant pillow.
Nobody touched the ground.
Nobody *fell*.
Then the white dummies started turning *colors*.
A blue arm here.
A red nose there.
BlastKid_7 dropped the giant hand.
He pressed the *Heal* button instead.
Then he tried to hug us.
We all flopped over.
Onto each other, of course.
Turns out the rule was only half written.
Every catch gives the color *back*.

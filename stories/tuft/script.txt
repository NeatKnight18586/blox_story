In this world, you get whatever power your *beast* has.
Vance's dragon breathes *cyan fire*, so now Vance can too.
A storm wolf makes this girl faster than *lightning*.
A sky hawk lets this kid *fly*.
Then the teacher points at me.
My beast, Tuft, is the size of a *muffin*.
He's scared of everything.
So am I.
But by tomorrow night, a *Titan* will run from us.
A spider drops onto my desk.
Tuft puffs up.
And so do *I*.
I bounce off the ceiling like a beach ball.
Everyone laughs.
But nobody sees Vance's dragon back away from me.
Vance says, "Enjoy your *balloon* power."
"At the festival, I'm getting the strongest beast alive."
At dinner, my little sister Juni feeds Tuft blueberries.
Dad's stone ox lifts our table for fun.
Then the toaster pops.
I puff.
The table *flies*.
Dad's ox is now wearing spaghetti.
Juni whispers, "Tuft picked you for a reason."
I wish he picked a braver kid.
That night, thunder cracks over the village.
I puff so big I fill my whole room.
My legs stick out the *window*.
And every beast on our street runs away.
I tell Tuft I wish he was a dragon.
He hides under my bed all day.
Then comes the Beast Festival.
Lanterns, music, and every beast in the village.
Vance pulls a cloth off a giant cage.
Inside is the *Titan* from the mountain.
"Its power is mine now," he says.
But the rule says *your* beast.
And the Titan was never his.
Vance gets nothing.
And the Titan gets *angry*.
The chains snap.
Every brave beast in the village runs.
Even the *dragon*.
And Juni is frozen in its path.
Tuft jumps from my hoodie and runs to her.
So I run after him.
I have never been this scared in my whole life.
The rule said *whatever* power.
And Tuft's power grows with how scared you are.
Tuft never needed a brave kid.
He needed the *most scared* one.
We puff.
And puff.
Past the roofs, past the lanterns, past the *Titan*.
The Titan looks up at us.
Then it runs all the way home.
We deflate with the loudest *squeak* in history.
Juni hugs Tuft, then me.
Vance's dragon sleeps behind my house now.
And Juni says *boo* to me every night.
Just in case.

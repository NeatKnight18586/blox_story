# Town kit — what matters for our Shorts

Derived from the reference Short (Waterville) and Roblox town-building practice (mixed surface materials, trim, overhangs, window boxes/shutters, landscaping, props).

## What actually matters on a 9:16 phone frame
1. **Facades, not buildings.** Cameras see fronts. Every module is an 8-deep shell with its front on z=0; interiors, roofs and back walls nobody sees cost nothing.
2. **Colour-block per building.** One saturated pastel per facade (brick red, mustard, mint, sky, pink) plus white trim. This lifts the frame more than any single detail.
3. **Detail at character height (0–10 studs).** Doors, shop windows, awnings, signs, flower boxes, lamps, benches, kerbs: that is what surrounds characters in medium shots. Upper floors only need windows and shutters.
4. **Surface materials, not flat colour.** Brick, siding, stone, plaster, planks, paving, shingles, all painted patterns tinted per building, with repeat sized per face so bricks never stretch.
5. **Depth layers.** Foreground prop (lamp/tree/hydrant), characters, facades, skyline blocks, sky. Fog and the sky gradient make the far layer recede.
6. **Ground fills half the frame.** Bare paving killed the old wide. Use tile patterns, the ringed plaza, road with lane dashes, crossings and kerbs.
7. **Crowd from the same rig.** Seeded extras: same six blocks, painted tees, hoodies and stripes, the eight hair options, Roblox-palette skin. Seeded means a shot re-renders identically.

## What does not matter (don't build it)
- Interiors behind facades, full city grids, physics, traffic, day/night systems.
- Unique one-off buildings: variety comes from module parameters (width, wall material, colours, sign text).

## Scale (Roblox-like)
Character ≈ 5.2 tall; door 7; ground floor 10; shop 19 + parapet; house 13 + gable; clock tower 28 + roof; road 12 wide; sidewalks 6.

## Known gaps
- Wide shots measure ~70 colourfulness (gate 80): the grey road and pale paving still dominate. Options: warmer road tone, coloured crossing/bike lane, more greenery strips.
- No walking crowd yet (static poses); motion comes with the native sequence stage.

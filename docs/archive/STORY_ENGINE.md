# STORY ENGINE: how every episode is built

Owner decision 2026-11-20. Format numbers live in [FORMAT_V6.md](FORMAT_V6.md); brand in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md); the per-episode
build input is [EPISODE_BRIEF.md](EPISODE_BRIEF.md). `STORY_BIBLE.md` is superseded by this page except its banned-rules list.

1. **The rule is a feeling in disguise.** Write the feeling first ("never being hugged", "parents never listen"), then the rule
   that turns it into a visible game mechanic. One sentence, ≤ 9 words after "In this world,". Test line: a 10-year-old AND
   their parent both react to it.
2. **Everyday gags.** Three quick gags show the rule in ordinary life (school, dinner, home), each happening to the hero or his
   family, each bigger than the last. One of them plants the loophole clue.
3. **The hero's weakness.** Under this rule the hero is the worst-placed kid because of one flaw. That flaw is what saves the day.
4. **A named loved one in danger.** A specific person (sibling, parent, best friend, grandparent), named and on screen before
   0:40, in visible danger at the climax. Never "the town" alone.
5. **The loophole (our brand).** The rescue comes from the rule's exact wording. The clue is planted early and shown twice, the
   second time slightly wider. A sharp viewer could solve it first.
6. **The ending.** The last line twists the rule's wording or lands the last laugh: a callback gag or a quiet emotional image.
   Never a recap (FORMAT_V6 rule 3).
7. **The comment question.** Every episode ships with one question viewers answer about themselves ("What colour would you
   be?"). It goes in the pinned comment.
8. **Escalation + open loops.** A new open question every 15–20 s; never close one without opening the next. Each setback is
   bigger than the last.
9. **Steal like an artist.** Take methods, never surfaces. Allowed sources: idioms taken literally, invisible things made
   visible, public-domain fables/riddles/myths reskinned, real facts turned into rules, other creators' story ENGINES.
   Forbidden: another creator's rule, names, signature images, twists or titles. Every premise must pass:
   (a) "Could a viewer say 'that's the X one'?" = no; (b) not in STORY_BIBLE.md's banned list; (c) mixes at least two unrelated
   sources. Seeds: [SWIPE_FILE.md](SWIPE_FILE.md).
